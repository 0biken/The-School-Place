Frequently Asked Questions

We primarily focus on the Cambridge International A-Level curriculum, as our tutors are Cambridge assessors. However, we also support students taking Edexcel and other major exam boards.

Sessions are conducted one-on-one via video call with an interactive whiteboard. We focus heavily on past paper practice and exam technique.

Resit support is highly targeted. We identify exactly where you lost marks in your previous attempt and drill exam technique, time management, and specific knowledge gaps to ensure a higher grade.

We understand schedules can change. We ask for 24 hours notice for any rescheduling to ensure we can adjust our tutors' calendars accordingly.
